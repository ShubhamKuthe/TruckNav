Windows Companion Software · UI Design

TruckNav Connector

The PC half of TruckNav — a quiet background utility that finds the phone on the local network, captures the GPS area out of Euro Truck Simulator 2 or American Truck Simulator, and streams it across. Same dark, amber-accented language as the Android app, rebuilt as a compact native Windows tool — not a stretched phone screen.
